# UX & Screen Real-Estate Overhaul
- Progressive Disclosure: Two-phase layout (Phase 1 Hero Stage for Attempt 0, Phase 2 Codebreaker's Desk for Attempt 1+).
- Hero Stage: Prominent quote typography, wandering animated cows and bulls, and pasture selector before first guess.
- Merged Input Bar: 4-digit cards and "Lock In" button unified into a single horizontal bar.
- Collapsible Keypad: iPad-like dock with collapse button [⌵] and auto-collapse on board scroll.
- Chronological Auto-Scroll: Clean top-to-bottom deduction journal with rock-solid auto-scroll (no sort toggles to avoid clutter).
- Header Quote Dialog: Tappable title [🐮 Cows & Bulls 🐂] opens comic banter sheet between Cow & Bull, dropping the separate 🌾 button to prevent title line wraps.
