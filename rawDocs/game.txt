# Cows and Bulls
----------------

This is a guessing game. Typically played by two players.
Let's call them player and challenger. The challenger picks a 
4-digit number without revealing to the player and the player 
guesses the number. The challenger and player can take turns
and switch roles.

Players use 4 unique (no repeated) digits from 0 to 9. (Leading 0 is
allowed since it is treated as a 4-character code rather than a
mathematical number)

# Rules
-------

1. The player has 20 attempts, else they lose.
2. The player can concede early and they lose. Quit is also concede.
3. Secret is revealed on surrender.

# Game in Play
--------------
1. Guessing player guesses the 4 digits.
2. For every digit that is present in the number, but in wrong place the challeneger says Cow. e.g. 1 Cow, 2 Cows, 3 Cows, 4 Cows.
3. For every digit that is present in the number and in right place the challeneger says Bull. e.g. 1 Bull, 2 Bulls, 3 Bulls, 4 Bulls.
3. 4 Bulls is a victory to the guessing player.
